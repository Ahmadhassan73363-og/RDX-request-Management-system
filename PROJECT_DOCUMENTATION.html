What is the Request Tracking Utility?
Plain-English summary of the business problem and how this software solves it
Core Purpose: Companies lose thousands of dollars each month when team members give away free product samples, client gifts, or promotional items without budget checks or manager approvals. RDX Request Tracking Utility automates this entire lifecycle so that no sample leaves the warehouse without authorized signatures and recorded budget deductions.

Before this system, requests were handled through scattered emails, paper slips, or spreadsheets. This created 3 major bottlenecks:

❌ Problem 1: Budget Leakage
Teams gave away samples without knowing if their department still had budget left, causing unplanned deficits.
❌ Problem 2: Lost Approvals
Requests got stuck in executive inboxes for weeks with zero tracking or visibility into who was holding them up.
❌ Problem 3: Warehouse Blindspot
Once approved, no one knew if warehouse staff actually packed, dispatched, or delivered the items to the client.
✅ The RDX Solution:
A single connected dashboard where Sales/Staff create requests → Executives digitally sign in a strict 4-step sequence → Budgets auto-deduct → Warehouse staff fulfill shipments on a real-time Kanban board → Every detail is audited with timestamps and IP stamps.

Frontend Tier
React 19 & TypeScript
Fast, reactive Single Page Application (SPA) powered by Vite and styled with TailwindCSS.
  • 12 modular feature pages (Dashboard, Requests, Shipments, etc.)
  • Interactive digital signature canvas for approvals
  • Instant local response via optimistic updates
Backend Tier
Express 5 REST API
High-throughput Node.js backend providing standardized RESTful JSON endpoints.
  • Single fast-bootstrap bundle route (/api/bootstrap)
  • Non-destructive automatic schema validation (initDb.js)
  • Warm lambda connection pooling to prevent pool exhaustion
Database Tier
PostgreSQL on Neon Cloud
ACID-compliant relational database ensuring 100% financial and audit integrity.
  • 12 structured tables with relational foreign keys
  • JSONB support for dynamic forms & multi-SKU arrays
  • Immutable transaction ledger for every dollar spent

The 4-Stage Approval Workflow
How a sample request travels through the corporate hierarchy

Every request must successfully pass through 4 sequential executive authorization levels. If rejected at any stage, it stops immediately.

Stage 1 of 4
👤 Executive Review
Reviews business justification, item SKU breakdown, and client relationship history.
Stage 2 of 4
👥 Manager Review
Verifies team-level operational necessity and validates delivery deadlines.
Stage 3 of 4
🏢 HOD Review
Checks department spending, budget allocations, and compliance rules.
Stage 4 of 4
👑 President Final Sign-Off
Grants final company sanction. Triggers automatic budget deduction and fulfillment.

Warehouse & Shipment Fulfillment Tracking
Real-time Kanban logistics pipeline after request approval

Once President signs off, the request automatically enrolls into the Shipment Tracking dashboard. Warehouse managers move orders through 4 physical states:

Phase 1 🟢
Approved – Ready
Authorized by President. Enqueued for warehouse pickup and allocation.
Phase 2 📦
In Process
Warehouse team is packaging, labeling, and performing quality inspection.
Phase 3 🚚
Dispatched
Package handed over to courier. Waybill & tracking notes attached.
Phase 4 ✅
Delivered
Delivery confirmed by client or recipient. Timestamp recorded. Closed.

What happens if the internet goes down?
The user's work is never lost. The action is held securely in the browser's local storage, and a subtle warning badge notifies them that cloud sync will resume when the connection is restored.
