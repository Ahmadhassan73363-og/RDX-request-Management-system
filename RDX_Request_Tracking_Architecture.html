What is the Request Tracking Utility?
Plain-English summary of the business problem and how this software solves it
Core Purpose: Companies lose thousands of dollars each month when team members give away free product samples, client gifts, or promotional items without budget checks or manager approvals. RDX Request Tracking Utility automates this entire lifecycle so that no sample leaves the warehouse without authorized signatures and recorded budget deductions.
Before this system, requests were handled through scattered emails, paper slips, or spreadsheets. This created 3 major bottlenecks:
❌ Problem 1: Budget Leakage
Teams gave away samples without knowing if their department still had budget left, causing unplanned deficits.
❌ Problem 2: Lost Approvals
Requests got stuck in executive inboxes for weeks with zero tracking or visibility into who was holding them up.
❌ Problem 3: Warehouse Blindspot
Once approved, no one knew if warehouse staff actually packed, dispatched, or delivered the items to the client.
✅ The RDX Solution:
A single connected dashboard where Sales/Staff create requests → Executives digitally sign in a strict 4-step sequence → Budgets auto-deduct → Warehouse staff fulfill shipments on a real-time Kanban board → Every detail is audited with timestamps and IP stamps.

The 4-Stage Approval Journey
How a sample request travels through the corporate hierarchy
STAGE 1 Executive
Commercial Check:
Reviews why the sample is needed, client business history, and specific items requested.
STAGE 2 Manager
Team Alignment:
Confirms operational necessity, sales pipeline priority, and required delivery target dates.
STAGE 3 HOD
Budget Authority:
Validates that the department has enough remaining money and adheres to gift caps.
STAGE 4 President
Final Sign-Off:
Grants final corporate green light. Auto-deducts team budget and queues shipment.
CALCULATION How Pricing Is Calculated
• Request Value: Total retail value of sample items.
• Formula: $\text{Budget Amount} = \text{Value} \times (1 - \frac{\text{Discount}}{100})$
• Hard Stop: Blocks submission if department money is low.
• Override Flag: Super Admins can override, flagged for audit.
EXECUTION Instant Budget Deduction
• Instant Debit: When President signs, money is auto-deducted.
• Receipt Created: Writes a new row in budget_transactions.
• Total Clarity: Records balance_before and balance_after.
• Zero Disputes: Links exact order number to the deduction.

Warehouse Fulfillment & Courier Tracking
Real-time Kanban logistics pipeline after request approval
Closing the Loop: Approving a sample is only half the job. The Shipment Tracking Kanban board gives warehouse staff and sales agents full visibility into packaging, courier pickup, and confirmed customer delivery.
🟢 1. Approved – Ready
President signed off. Order is queued for warehouse stock picking and allocation.
📦 2. In Process
Warehouse team is packing, labeling shipping boxes, and inspecting sample quality.
🚚 3. Dispatched
Package handed over to courier. Waybill number and tracking notes are attached.
✅ 4. Delivered
Delivery confirmed by client or recipient. System records timestamp and closes request.
